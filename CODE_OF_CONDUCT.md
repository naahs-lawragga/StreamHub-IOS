# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and users with respect. Welcome people of every background and experience level. Discuss ideas and evidence without personal attacks. Keep criticism constructive and respect privacy.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, publishing private information, and deliberate disruption are unacceptable. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community. Enforcement should be proportionate and explain the behavior involved.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile contact options. Do not publish sensitive personal information in an issue. Maintainers should recuse themselves when directly involved in a report.
