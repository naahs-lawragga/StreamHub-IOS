## JustWatch

TMDB viewing-provider results are powered by JustWatch. The provider screen attributes JustWatch and opens TMDB's required watch-provider link. Results describe region-specific title listings, not confirmed subscriptions, episode-specific availability, or playable media URLs.

https://developer.themoviedb.org/reference/movie-watch-providers

## FMHY

The optional directory fetches and parses the public upstream Markdown wiki at runtime. No copy of the directory is bundled or relicensed under MIT. Cached directory entries retain the upstream authors' rights. The app identifies and links to the upstream source. Directory entries are unverified websites, not evidence of rights or title availability.

https://github.com/fmhy/FMHY/wiki/Streaming
